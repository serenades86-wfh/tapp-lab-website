Connecting research with better mental health care.

The Translational AI for Precision Psychiatry (TAPP) Lab at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH) brings together approaches from data science, psychology, psychiatry and neuroscience. Our research explores how findings and methods can move from research into clinical practice, with the aim of supporting meaningful, responsible improvements in mental health care.
