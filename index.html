01 / What we study

Our research

Connecting clinical knowledge, biological data and machine learning to support better decisions in psychiatry.

01 / PRECISION CARE

Personalized treatment

Understanding and predicting psychiatric treatment response, side effects, non-response and discontinuation.

Learn more ↗
02 / CLINICAL TRANSLATION

AI that works in practice

Translating predictive models from research settings into real-world clinical data and decision-support workflows.

Learn more ↗
03 / RESPONSIBLE METHODS

Fair, useful, human-centred AI

Evaluating feasibility, fairness, patient preferences and the real-world value of AI-powered tools.

Learn more ↗
data → care
From models to meaningful impact

Research with real-world purpose.

Our focus is not only on developing predictive models, but also on understanding whether they can meaningfully support clinical care. The lab explores the translation, validation and implementation of AI tools that respond to the needs of patients and clinicians.
